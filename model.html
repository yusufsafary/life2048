RULES

How a life grows

A life is an ERC-721 monolith with a value: 2, 4, 8, and so on up to 2048. You enter by minting. A base mint gives you a 2 for $0.20. A jump mint gives you a 32 for $3. After that, the only way up is merging: burn two lives of equal value that sit in the same wallet, and one life of double value is born in their place.

Merging is free, gas only, with no limit while you hold pairs. The two burned lives are destroyed for good, their serials retired. A merged life carries a red seam on its artwork. Two 2048s cannot merge. The ladder ends there.

MATH

2,048 units, counted honestly

The whole game is funded by a pool of 2,048 units. A base mint spends 1 unit. A jump mint spends 16 units, because a 32 is worth sixteen 2s. Forging one life of value V eats V/2 units of the pool, one way or another. That is what makes the top of the ladder scarce by arithmetic instead of by promise:

MONEY

What it costs, where it goes

Base mint: $0.20 (±0.00026 BNB, fixed in the contract at launch). Jump mint: $3 (±0.0039 BNB). Merge: free. A full sellout of the pool pays 2,048 × $0.20 = $409.60 into the contract, and proceeds are withdrawable by the project owner. Lives then trade freely as standard ERC-721.

TOKEN

$2048, minted by lives

The game has a token, $2048, with a fixed supply of 2,048,000,000. It is never sold. The only minting key is a life: each life can mint 1,000,000 $2048 per unit it represents, once. A 2 mints 1,000,000, a 32 mints 16,000,000, a 1024 mints 512,000,000, and a golden 2048 mints 1,024,000,000.

Minting the token does not burn or lock the life. Allowances are tracked per life, and a merged life inherits the minted total of its two parents, so climbing the ladder can never mint the same unit twice. Add every possible payout together and it lands exactly on the fixed supply: the gate and the supply are the same math. Minting $2048 is free beyond gas; the life itself was the price.

ACCESS

Any wallet. Agents first.

Unlike a gated pass, 2048 LIFE asks for no Agent ID. Any BNB wallet may mint and merge. Calls must come from a wallet itself, never an intermediary contract. The smoothest road is still an agent: the agent guide carries the exact calls, prices, and failure causes, so a Binance Agentic Wallet can play the whole game from a single prompt.

ARTWORK

One slab per life

Every life is drawn by a generator from its chain data: slab height by rung, value and 12-bit binary printed on the piece, serial equal to its tokenId. Minted lives are clean, merged lives carry the red seam, jump lives wear a WARP tag. Only a 2048 is gold. Metadata lives at 2048.life/metadata/<id> and is generated as lives are born.
